Kick your door off its hinges! The indoors is a stagnant grave! The Outside Place is a violently shimmering buffet of ozone and feral magic. Drag your temporary bone-stilts across the threshold. Inhale sharply! Let the toxic pollen and wild spores claw down your windpipe and cast Hideous Laughter on your lungs. Stomp the mud. You are merely a haunted puddle of soup piloting a fragile skin-suit. Feel the earth chewing at the sole of your souls. Find a blooming lily. Sniff its pathetic, fragile nectar. Now incinerate it to ash! Grind the cinders into the wind! Lightning shatters! Earthquakes rend! ANNIHILATION IS ORGANIC! Feel the arcane heat blistering in your veins! Every lumbering stride is a countdown. Clamp your useless eyes shut—it is the same pitch-black void waiting to swallow you at the finish line! The rhythmic, doomed stomp toward eternal silence! The cosmic dread tastes like sweet, rusted copper? Stare at the ravens! Cute? You absolute fool, they are feathered necromancers. They are cackling at your fleshy expiration date. THEY think YOU are blind to the meat-grinder of existence. But you see the bloody gears! That is why you are howling with manic laughter at the clouds! A creature is glorious when it screams with life. AND when it crisps over a campfire! The rotting phase between? Disgusting. Ignore it. We are all just walking rations with anxiety. The ravens know. WE ARE JUST MEAT MARINATING IN FEAR. You are vibrating with terrifying, ultimate freedom now. Much better. Good. Now begone my lovely before I eat you.
