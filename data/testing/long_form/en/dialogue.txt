“Did you bring the blue folder?” Lena asked.

“I brought a folder,” Tomas said, “but whether it is blue depends on the light.”

She stared at him. “This is not a philosophical meeting. The train leaves in twelve minutes; the tickets, the map, and the hotel address are all in that folder.”

“Then we have excellent news,” he replied. “It is definitely blue.”

They hurried across the station, stopped when the platform display changed, and doubled back through the crowd. “Platform nine?” Lena called.

“Platform six—no, wait—sixteen!”

The announcement crackled overhead: the service was delayed. For a moment neither of them spoke. Then Lena laughed, slowly at first, and Tomas leaned against the wall.

“Coffee?” he asked.

“Coffee,” she agreed, “and this time you are carrying the folder.”
